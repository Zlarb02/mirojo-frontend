How It Works

What is Mini Roleplay Journey?

A quick, immersive, and customizable RPG experience for players who want engaging stories in just 5 minutes.

Do I need to create a character?

You can create a character from scratch or pick from preset universes full of characters, stories, and interactions.

Is it multiplayer?

Yes! You can collaborate with friends for a rich roleplaying experience.
